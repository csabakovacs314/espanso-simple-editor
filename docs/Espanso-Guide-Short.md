# Espanso and Espanso Simple Editor

> **In short**
> Espanso types your templates for you: type `/jira` and the full text appears. Espanso Simple Editor lets you manage those templates in a simple window, like PhraseExpress.

## What is Espanso?

A free tool that types text for you. Type a short code like `/jira` and it is replaced with your full template.

## Why not PhraseExpress?

* **Free** for work use (PhraseExpress needs a licence)
* **Light**, runs in the background
* **Snippets are simple files**, easy to back up and share

## How to start

1. Install Espanso from https://espanso.org/install/
2. Type `:espanso` anywhere. If it changes to `Hi there!`, it works.
3. Press `Alt+Space` to search all your snippets.

## Espanso Simple Editor

Espanso normally needs its snippets written in special text files. **Espanso Simple Editor** gives you a simple window instead.

* Your snippets on the left, the text on the right
* **Bold, bullets and headings** for Jira Cloud and Outlook
* Add a date, the clipboard or a question with the **Insert** button
* `Ctrl+S` to save. Espanso picks it up straight away, and a backup is kept.
